## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Full-Cycle Election Transmission (US Primaries + Mid-Terms + Presidential & India Lok Sabha + State Polls)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Model election impact as a continuous multi-stage vector rather than a single day event. **US Primaries** act as an early-warning volatility & rhetoric filter on NSE IT (`TCS`, `INFY`) and Pharma (`SUNPHARMA`, `DRREDDY`, `CIPLA`), **US Mid-Terms/Presidential** drive actual tariff, Biosecure Act, INDUS-X defense, and 10Y Treasury yield shifts, while **India Lok Sabha & State Elections** govern domestic PSU, Defence (`HAL`, `BEL`), Infra (`LT`), and Rural FMCG (`ITC`, `HINDUNILVR`) capital allocation.
  - *Why*: Institutional FII/DII flows re-price sectors months ahead during US Primaries and India pre-poll Model Code of Conduct windows—waiting until general election results misses the bottom accumulation and early Put hedging windows.
- **Decision 2: Dual-Target (`T1 / T2 / T3`) Non-Erosion Ratchet State Machine**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Every 52W-Low Accumulation setup and Bear Put (`PE`) F&O setup defines three progressive targets (`T1` conservative `+2.4%` to `+3.2%` net, `T2` structural breakout `+4.8%` to `+6.5%`, `T3` full trend extension `+8.5%+`). Passing `T1` ratchets the hard stop to lock `T1` net profit; passing `T2` ratchets the stop to lock `T2` net profit; approaching `T3` trails at `82%` of high-water-mark P&L.
  - *Why*: Prevents winning bottom-fishing or Put option trades from round-tripping into losses when volatile markets bounce.
- **Decision 3: Immutable Before/After Statutory Tax Versioning on Orders**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Stamp each executed order with `taxRuleVersionId` and its exact `statutoryFrictionSnapshot` at execution time. When a T-1 rule change is approved by the user, only subsequent evaluations and orders use the new tax/brokerage rates.
  - *Why*: Guarantees that historical realized P&L and net-of-tax audits are never retroactively distorted by future STT, SEBI, or brokerage fee changes.
