# J.A.R.V.I.S. Institutional Quant Terminal — Core Constitution & Directives

## 1. ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION OF DUMMY / DEMO / MOCK DATA
Under no circumstances should dummy, mock, simulated, or pre-fabricated placeholder data be injected into the terminal dashboards, including the 5-Agent Desk:

1. **Capital Allocation**:
   - Never hardcode arbitrary portfolio funds (e.g. ₹2.5 Crore, ₹1 Crore, ₹50 Lakh).
   - Capital allocations must be directly configured by the user/owner via allocation controls, or linked directly to verified connected broker margins.
   - Initial state when unconfigured must be ₹0 or explicitly marked as `Unallocated`.

2. **Realized & Unrealized P&L**:
   - Realized P&L must strictly be ₹0.00 until an actual trade order in the session is squared off/closed.
   - Unrealized P&L must strictly be ₹0.00 until an actual position is opened, and thereafter must be mathematically derived from `(liveMarketPrice - entryPrice) * quantity`.
   - Never inject arbitrary fake positive P&L (e.g. +₹420, +₹650, +₹118,400).

3. **Win Rates & Trade Counts**:
   - When 0 trades have been closed in a session, win rate must display `--` or `N/A` (never fake numbers like 88.9% or 91.2%).
   - Trades count must start at exactly `0`.

4. **Session Order Book**:
   - Starts completely clean (`[]`) with an empty state: "No active working orders in current session."
   - Never auto-populate fake historical or executed orders on boot. Orders only exist when generated by a live market scan or dispatched by the user.

5. **Live Market Prices & Strikes**:
   - All quotes, option strikes, and risk caps must be derived in real time from live exchange ticks (`/api/market-data`).

---

## 2. MANDATORY USER CONSULTATION BEFORE DESIGN & DEPLOYMENT
1. **Always Ask Before Replacing Core Modules**:
   - The engineer must never unilaterally design, overhaul, or deploy replacements for primary visualization or decision workspaces without first consulting the user.
   - The user must explicitly confirm the chosen analytical framework, data presentation, and interaction flow before code is written.

2. **No Purely Decorative 3D Gimmicks**:
   - 3D point clouds, rotating meshes, or abstract particle fields that do not offer actionable, trade-executing decisions are strictly forbidden.
   - Every visualization must function as an operational decision maker with explicit trade signals, entry bands, stop losses, profit targets, and risk parameters.
